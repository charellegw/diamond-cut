History

Founded in 1998, DiamondCut Motors has established itself as a leader in the luxury car resale market in Indonesia. Our journey began with a passion for luxury vehicles and a commitment to delivering exceptional customer service. Over the years, we have built a reputation for excellence, earning the trust of business owners, successful individuals, and car enthusiasts alike. In the early days, our founders traveled the world to hand-select the finest luxury vehicles, bringing them back to Indonesia to share with our discerning clients. As our reputation grew, so did our network of suppliers and partners, allowing us to expand our offerings to include the world's most prestigious brands, such as Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, and Bugatti. Today, DiamondCut Motors is proud to operate a network of luxurious showrooms across Indonesia, showcasing our extensive collection of luxury vehicles. Our team of experts is dedicated to providing personalized service, ensuring that every client finds their perfect vehicle. With a focus on innovation and customer satisfaction, we continue to push the boundaries of what is possible in the luxury car resale market. As we look to the future, we are committed to expanding our reach globally, leveraging technology to enhance the car buying experience, and continuing to deliver exceptional service to our valued clients.
